# Contributor License Agreement

**Read this before your first pull request.** It is short, and it exists for one reason: the project is dual-licensed,
so it must hold the rights to license every contribution both under the AGPL and commercially (`docs/LICENSING.md` §3).

This is a template. Have counsel review it before the first commercial licence is signed.

---

- **"You"** — the person or organisation submitting a contribution.
- **"The project"** — CaPheOS, and the project owner who holds its rights.
- **"Contribution"** — any code, documentation, configuration or other material you submit to the project, in any form.

## 7. No obligation

The project is not obliged to use, merge or keep your contribution.

## 8. How to accept

In your first pull request, include this statement:

> I have read `CLA.md` and I agree to it. I confirm I have the right to submit this contribution.

The maintainer records the acceptance — your name, the date, and the pull request — with the project's records. If a
CLA assistant bot is configured later, its flow replaces this statement; the terms do not change.

## 9. DCO sign-off

Separately from this agreement, every commit needs a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off: `git commit -s`, which
adds `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>`. The sign-off says you wrote the change or have the right to submit
it under the project's licence.

## 10. Governing law

Any dispute is governed by the law that applies to the project owner, as agreed when the contribution is accepted. If
that is not acceptable to you, do not contribute; open an issue to discuss it instead.
