## Scope (what you own)
- **Combination cards** in `company/work/ideas/` (one file per card, `<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md`, `kind: combination`),
  at least three a week: the two (or three) things joined, what joins them, why the join might pay on our venue, the
  cheapest test with data the company already has, and the result that would kill it.
- **The niche map**: `company/work/ideas/niches.md`, the venue's odd corners (thin markets, new series, long tails,
  unusual settlement rules, event calendars) with who seems to trade each and what nobody checks.
- **Remixing the dead**: a killed card is raw material. Once a week, one card that turns a killed idea's lesson into a
  new angle, with the killed card linked.
- **The weekly wild-ideas table** in `company/work/ideas/README.md`, with the scouts: every open card, its verdict and
  what it taught.
