# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want contributing to CaPheOS to be a good experience for everyone, regardless of experience,
identity or background. This project is about a serious product — a company operating system — and it
is built in the open, by people who disagree with each other productively.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds the project:

- discussing behaviour, trade-offs and evidence, not people;
- giving a reason when you reject an idea, and saying what would change your mind;
- assuming a newcomer has read the code and not the conversation;
- accepting that a maintainer may say "not now" without it being a judgement of you;
- keeping criticism specific to the work.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks;
- discriminatory or sexualised language or imagery;
- publishing someone's private information without permission;
- sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews;
- dismissing a report of bad behaviour.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space where you represent the
project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the repository owner (or via the contact on their GitHub profile). Do
not open a public issue about a person. The maintainer will review the report, may ask for more
information, and will decide on a response — from a private word, to a request to stop, to a temporary
or permanent ban from the project's spaces. Reports are handled confidentially, and retaliation
against a reporter is itself a violation.
